Paper trading research for autonomous market agents.

Atlas Agents helps run local paper-trading simulations, inspect agent decisions, review logs, and study strategy performance.

Atlas Agents is not a broker, investment adviser, bank, custodian, money transmitter, or payment processor. The current product is paper-trading and research only.

Paper First

Signals, portfolio changes, and trades are simulated unless a future release explicitly enables real-money integrations.

Observable

Agent actions are logged so every signal, risk check, and simulated order can be reviewed.

Private Use

The current deployment is intended for personal research and internal evaluation, not public investment services.
